Incoming inspection

  • Check by eye that the solder mask really is absent around the input pads J1 and J2. This is the most frequent defect, and the most silent.
  • Check that the slot in the ground plane is clean and continuous, on both visible sides.
  • With an ohmmeter, check the isolation between the two ground planes: it must be infinite.
  • Check flatness: a warped board makes placing the QFN packages a matter of luck.
