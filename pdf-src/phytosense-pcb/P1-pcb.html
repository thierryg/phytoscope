Printed circuit board — PhytoSense One

Four layers, one hundred by sixty millimetres, 1.6 mm FR4, 35 µm copper, ENIG finish. This fascicle holds the routing plates in landscape format and the fabrication package. The explanations are in chapter 8 of the book.

⚠️ The three absolute rules

1. The guard ring surrounds the input on all four layers. 2. Solder mask is kept clear over the input area, and that option has to be asked for explicitly when ordering. 3. No trace crosses the slot in the ground plane — a single one would be enough to defeat the isolation without stopping the board from working.
