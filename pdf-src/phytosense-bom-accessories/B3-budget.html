⚠️ The line item everybody forgets

It is neither a component nor a tool: it is time. Fifteen hours of assembly, three hours of acceptance testing, and an hour or so a year to redo the acceptance sheet. On top of that comes the learning, which is not wasted time but is time all the same. A commercial instrument is unboxed and works; this one is built. The choice depends on what you want to do, not on what you want to own.
