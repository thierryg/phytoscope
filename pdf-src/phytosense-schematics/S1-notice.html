Schematic package — PhytoSense One

This fascicle gathers the eight schematic sheets of the PhytoSense One board, in landscape format, to be printed and taken to the bench. It contains no explanations: those are in the book The PhytoSense Board, and each sheet says which chapter to turn to.

Conventions

Symbols follow IEC 60617. Analog ground is a solid triangle, digital ground a comb. The pentagonal labels mark inter-sheet signals. The copper dash-dot line is the galvanic isolation barrier: no conductor crosses it.

Before connecting anything

The board runs on USB 5 V, on a battery, or on an external 7 to 24 V supply at safety extra-low voltage. It has no part connected directly to the mains, and must never have one: isolating from the mains is the external supply's job, and that supply must carry CE marking and double insulation. Never connect an earthed oscilloscope to a rig connected to a living being. The 22 kΩ resistor in series with the reference electrode is a safety limitation: it is not a substitute for one.
